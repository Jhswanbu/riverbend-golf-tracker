⛳ Riverbend Scorekeeper

The whole field's scores, entered like the spreadsheet. Anyone can enter and submit a round — no login needed; it counts right away, and an admin is alerted for every submission. Admins and scorekeepers sign in to manage rounds. No live scoring.

Admin sign-in

You don't need an account to enter scores. Sign in (admins and scorekeepers) for Past Rounds, adding players, and removing rounds.

1 · Setup

Tap a player's name (or the chip) to cycle: — → Team 1 → Team 2 → —. Pick a tee-time group once a player is assigned, and check the tees they're playing from — W unless they're moved back to the Blues, Combos, or Reds. A tee you change here is remembered on that player's record. If a player leaves early, just leave the holes they didn't play blank — blank holes are recorded as left early, and they still owe (or collect) the full team amount for the round. Saving asks you to confirm any blanks before the round is recorded.

Add a player

Someone new playing today? Add them to the roster here — they can be put on a team right away. (Admins and scorekeepers only.)

2 · Bets & settings

Until you change a bet yourself, the defaults follow the team sizes — pairs of balls (1-2, 3-4, …) the smaller team can actually cover, so the app never invents a bet that doesn't exist. Once you edit the bets, team changes leave them alone.

3 · Scores

Par row is editable — change it when the group plays a different course.

4 · Results

Results appear here once every assigned player has a full card — or a partial card (blank holes = left early).

Upload Spreadsheet

First: in Excel, enter the scores and Save the file (so the totals are written), then pick that saved file here. On an iPad, work from the plain .xlsx copy — saving an .xlsm from iPad Excel strips its macros (and its desktop button), so never save over the .xlsm master.

The file is read on this device only — just the scores are sent to the tracker database, exactly like the standalone submit page.

Setup Card

The day's setup on one clean card — teams, groups, bets, and the All Balls holes. Take a picture of it to share with the group.
